# Phase 5 — Experiment G1.2: isotropic ν by GEOMETRIC DISTORTION alone (k=1 fixed)

**Question.** Achieve Goal 1 (isotropic Poisson ratio ν, E free) with **almost no k variation** — here
**k = 1 fixed for every bond**, so the *only* design variable is **vertex positions** — and see how much
of the isotropic ν range in [−1, 1] can be recovered by geometric **distortion**, across **10 genuinely
distinct triangulated topologies** (connectivity classes). "Topology" = connectivity class: a triangular
or *deformed*-triangular lattice is the **same** topology; a crystalline lattice with a basis is a
**different** topology. Every edge is a real uniform spring (no soft "fictional" edges — that would be
huge k-variation).

## Method
- **Design variable:** node positions only; **k ≡ 1**. **Connectivity frozen** per topology
  (`positions.spsa_positions(..., redelaunay_every=0)` — no re-Delaunay / no flips during a run).
- **Optimizer:** derivative-free SPSA on positions, started from a **symmetry-breaking jitter** (a
  perfect lattice is a stationary point → zero gradient → SPSA stalls; jitter gives it a direction),
  2 restarts spanning step size; keep the closest-to-target design that is **trustworthy** (independent
  full-PBC sim vs solver `gap < 0.05`), else the lowest-gap one.
- **Targets:** 11 isotropic ν in [−0.95 … +0.9], flat ν(θ); E left free (E-weight 0). Isotropy tracked
  via std of ν(θ) (`nu_aniso_std`, small throughout).
- **Verification:** every kept design independently simulated (a different code path from the solver).
- A geometry **health gate** screens each distorted candidate before the sim, because a near-singular
  distortion HARD-CRASHES the scipy/LAPACK sim (native segfault, uncatchable in Python).
- Sleep-prevention enabled (a prior overnight run was killed by system sleep).

**Run:** 110 designs (10 topologies × 11 ν); **45 clear the solver-vs-sim agreement gate**, and all
110 are reported — a rejected design is data, not an absence.

## Reachable isotropic ν per topology
Reference: eta-disordered triangular lattices (uniform k), **frozen 6-6-6 connectivity, Phase-2
convention** (`build_periodic_tf_mesh`: triangulate-first, displace every vertex by a random-direction
vector of magnitude exactly η, never re-triangulate, η<0.5), span ν ∈ **[−0.115, +0.327]** — falling
from +⅓ and crossing into **auxetic (~−0.1) as η→0.48**. (This corrects an earlier mistaken band of
[+0.26, +0.33], which used too-weak a displacement, capped η at 0.30, and wrongly re-triangulated.)
Note this reference is an UNFILTERED sim number — no gap test is applied to it — so it may only be
compared against the sim column below, never against the trustworthy window.

> **Read the two columns separately.** "Most auxetic reached" is what the network *does*: the
> independent sim's ν, with the solver's own ν beside it. "Trustworthy window" is the sub-range where
> the two code paths additionally agree to `gap < 0.05`. **They answer different questions**, and
> reporting only the second is what made an earlier version of this document claim that distortion
> never reaches negative ν.

**9 of the 10 topologies reach auxetic ν, and the solver and the sim agree on every one of them** —
all 43 negative designs are ν < 0 in BOTH code paths, median |ν_solver − ν_sim| = **0.008**, max 0.176
(`solver_recheck.npz`, produced by `g1_2_solver_recheck.py`). The most auxetic is honeycomb at
**ν = −0.436** (sim) / −0.488 (solver), with k identically 1 — far beyond the η-disorder band's −0.115.

`triangular` is the sole exception, and it is a **SELECTION failure — the optimiser found auxetic
designs and the code discarded them.** Measured directly by
`Phase 5/verifications/g1_2_triangular_start_probe.py` (36 runs, results in
`triangular_start_probe.csv`), which holds this experiment's exact budget and varies only the start:

| start | best ν reached | vs the η-disorder reference (−0.109) |
|---|---|---|
| `jitter_0.10` — **g1_2's own default** | **−0.134** | beats it |
| `jitter_0.30` | −0.123 | beats it |
| `eta_0.45` (start from an η-disordered configuration) | **−0.220** | doubles it |

So the start was never the problem, and neither was SPSA. **The controlling variable is the step
size `a`**: every run reaching deep negative ν used `a = 0.25`; the `a = 0.15` runs stayed positive
(+0.141 / +0.058 / +0.025 for seeds 0/1/2). Over the 120-step budget `a = 0.15` allows 1.61 lattice
spacings of travel per coordinate against 2.68 for `a = 0.25` — a third less reach.

Two consequences, and the second is the defect:
1. At `a = 0.15` SPSA barely moves and converges to a **target-independent** endpoint — identical
   results for ν* = −0.10 and ν* = −0.30. That is precisely the signature in the saved data: one
   byte-identical geometry (sha1 `3b5ca445f57d`) written for all five negative targets.
2. `design_one` keeps "the closest-to-target **trustworthy** design, falling back to the lowest-gap
   one if none is trustworthy". The timid `a = 0.15` design has a *small* gap (0.031 — it passes the
   gate); the `a = 0.25` design reaches ν = −0.134 but scores gap 0.371. **The rule therefore selects
   the design that did not do the job, because it is the one the two codes agree about.**

`jitter_0.30` is not a fix either: no deeper than the default, and far worse gaps (1.1–1.8).

> **This selection rule shapes every reach number in this experiment, not just `triangular`.** It is
> unchanged pending a deliberate decision on a criterion that balances target accuracy against
> solver-sim agreement instead of treating agreement as a veto.

> **Every negative-end reach in the table above is a LOWER bound.** Seven topologies (`triangular`,
> `flipped_tri_f8`, `kagome`, `rotating_squares`, `tetrakis`, `foam_poisson`, `honeycomb`) have
> clusters of negative targets sharing one saved geometry — the search stopping, not the material.
> They are marked ✗ in `fig1_reachable_by_topology.png`. The mirror-image clusters at ν* = +0.6/+0.9
> are different: those are trustworthy and sit at the ν ≈ +1/3 cap, i.e. genuine saturation.
>
> Note also that the η-disorder configuration reaching −0.109 **fails the gate itself** (gap 0.111)
> while its solver and sim agree on bulk ν to 0.001 — the same censoring described in the correction
> block, now demonstrated on a network the optimiser never touched.

Within the trustworthy window the reachable range is [+0.100, +0.342], median |ν − ν*| = 0.066.

## Key findings
> ### ⚠ CORRECTION 2026-08-21 — twice-corrected; read this before the numbers above
> **Round 1 (July → August).** The July run reported auxetic reach (−0.09 triangular, −0.21 `f14`)
> from data produced *before* the A-0 shear-contraction and A-10 ν-convention fixes. Those particular
> numbers are void.
>
> **Round 2 (the reporting error).** The first August rewrite of this document then concluded
> "no topology reaches negative ν". **That was wrong**, and wrongly propagated into `Phase 5/PLAN.md`
> and `validation_2026-08/MANIFEST.md`. It came from tabulating only rows that passed
> `trustworthy` — and `trustworthy` is *not* a physicality verdict. It is
> `gap = max_θ|Δν|/(|ν_sim|+0.05) + max_θ|ΔE|/|E_sim| < 0.05`: a **relative, per-angle agreement
> test between two code paths**. At |ν| ≈ 0.44 a 0.09 disagreement at ONE angle scores 0.19 and fails,
> while the bulk values agree to 0.03.
>
> **Measured truth:** 43 designs reach ν < 0, **all 43 auxetic in BOTH code paths**, median
> |ν_solver − ν_sim| = 0.008. The July *conclusion* was right even though its numbers were not.
> The figures now draw every design — filled = trustworthy, hollow = gap > 0.05 with a bar showing
> the disagreement — so this class of error cannot recur silently.

1. **Positions alone (uniform k, fixed connectivity) DO reach auxetic ν.** With k ≡ 1 exactly and
   connectivity frozen, distortion reaches **ν = −0.436** (honeycomb, independent sim; solver −0.488)
   and ν < 0 on **9 of 10** topologies. This is far beyond the random η-disorder reference, which only
   crosses zero at the singular edge (−0.115 at η = 0.48): **directed** distortion reaches a much
   deeper auxetic regime, and reaches it on demand — which is the point of optimizing over randomizing.
   What the honesty gate rejects is not the auxeticity; it is the *per-angle* agreement of the two
   codes on ν(θ) once |ν| is large.
2. **Universal upper cap ν ≈ +1/3.** Every topology stalls near +0.30–0.33 on the high side — the
   isotropic maximum cannot be raised by distortion, regardless of topology.
3. **Reach is topology-dependent; `honeycomb` and `square_octagon` go deepest.** The most auxetic
   reach belongs to `honeycomb` (−0.436) and `square_octagon` (−0.336) — both mechanism-prone
   structures with no trustworthy design at all, i.e. exactly where distortion buys the most ν and
   where the two codes agree least. The flipped variants are mid-pack (`f14` −0.294, `f8` −0.057), so
   the July claim that non-Delaunay flipping specifically opens the auxetic direction is not supported.
4. **The honesty gate marks where the two CODES diverge — not where the material stops.** Pushing ν
   further drives the network toward a near-mechanism, and there the solver's per-triangle metric
   solve and the sim's nodal solve stop agreeing on ν(θ): 65 of 110 runs are rejected. But the
   agreement they lose is *directional and relative* — on the rejected auxetic designs the two still
   agree on bulk ν to a median of 0.008 and never disagree in sign. **2** topologies
   (`reentrant_honeycomb`, `square_octagon`) have no design that clears the gate at all, and those two
   are also among the deepest-reaching — the correlation to note is gate-failure with *large* |ν|, not
   with unreality. Which code to believe there is open: the solver's per-triangle `A(s)` can lose rank
   near a mechanism (`CLAUDE.md` §3), while the sim linearises the kinematics. Neither is checked
   against a third path — that is what audit A-18's analytic oracle would settle.

## Limitations / next
- SPSA budget (2 restarts × 120 steps) is modest; more restarts would tighten reachable ranges and may
  recover some trustworthy designs on the "none" topologies.
- Extreme ν (|ν| → 1) is **not** reachable by distortion + uniform k — that regime needs stiffness
  contrast or genuine mechanisms (see Goal 1: contrast is the auxeticity lever).
- The mechanism-prone topologies would benefit from a small k-contrast allowance or a stability term.
